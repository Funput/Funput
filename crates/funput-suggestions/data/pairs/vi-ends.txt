# Vietnamese words whose last syllable also starts a line of vi.txt.
#
# Built by hand for Funput, from no external word list or corpus (see NOTICE.md).
#
# Predict reads the syllable before the caret. `cảm` starts `cảm ơn`, but in
# `dũng cảm` it ends a word, and offering `ơn` after that word is a wrong guess.
# Each line here is one such word: after it, the lines of vi.txt say nothing.
#
# Layout: one word per line, two syllables separated by one space, lowercase NFC,
# tone marks placed the modern way. Any `#` line is a comment; the words are
# grouped under the syllable they end with.
#
# What belongs here: a word people type, whose second syllable starts a line of
# vi.txt, and after which that line's followers would be wrong. Leave out:
# - a word vi.txt already spells as a line (`bưu điện`): the engine reads the
#   lines as words too;
# - a word the followers are still right after (`ngày hôm` → `nay`, `gọi điện`
#   → `thoại`).
#
# Every line is checked by `funput-lexicon-tool/tests/vi_pairs`.
